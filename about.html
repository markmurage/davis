HISTORY

Davis jaymo academy. was established in the year 2008
the founder JANE NYAWIRA MURAGE identified the need for a school that would provide quality education to children in the local community.
With a vision to create a nurturing and inclusive learning environment, the academy was founded with the aim of empowering students to reach their full potential.
In the year 2010, the academy expanded its facilities to accommodate a growing student population.
The school introduced innovative teaching methods and extracurricular activities to enhance the overall learning experience.

MISSION

Our mission is to provide a high-quality education that fosters academic excellence, personal growth, and social responsibility.
We are committed to creating an environment where every student can thrive and achieve their goals.

VISION

Our vision is to be a leading educational institution that establishes a strong education foundation and prepares students for success in a rapidly changing world.
We strive to cultivate a love for learning, critical thinking skills, and a sense of responsibility towards society.
By nurturing the talents and abilities of our students, we aim to empower them to become lifelong learners and responsible global citizens.
